Delete your TaskHaven account

Your TaskHaven account is on the Vikunja server you connected the app to (your own, or one run by someone you know), so it's that server that deletes it. You can ask it to, in the app, at any time.

In the app

  1. Open TaskHaven and sign in.
  2. Open the menu at your picture (top right) → Settings.
  3. Under Account, choose Delete account, type your password, and confirm.
  4. The server emails you a link: open it to confirm.

The server deletes the account three days after you confirm, and emails you a reminder before then. Until then you can keep it: Settings → Keep my account.

If the email doesn't come, or you can't use the app

Some servers can't send email. Ask whoever runs your server to delete your account (Vikunja has a command for it: vikunja user delete). TaskHaven's maker doesn't hold your account and can't delete it, but can help with questions about the app: [your contact email].

What's deleted

  • Your account: username, email, password and name.
  • The projects only you have, with all their tasks, comments and files, and your saved filters.
  • Your place in teams and in projects shared with you, your assignments and favourites.

What stays

  • Projects you shared with others stay theirs: one of them gets to manage each one.
  • What you wrote in projects shared with you (tasks, comments) stays with those projects.
  • Backups the server's owner keeps, as they keep them.

On your phone, signing out or removing the app deletes what the app kept there. More in the privacy policy.
