TaskHaven

To-do lists for you, and the people you choose

TaskHaven is an Android app for tasks and projects, kept on your own Vikunja server (at home, or on the internet at an https address). Your projects are yours alone until you share one with someone on the server: then just the two of you (or your family, or your club) see it, and nobody else.

TaskHaven is an independent app. It isn't made by, or connected with, the Vikunja project.
