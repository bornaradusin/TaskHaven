TaskHaven

Privacy policy

Last updated 23 October 2026

This explains what the TaskHaven app does with your data. In short: TaskHaven keeps your tasks on a Vikunja server that you choose (usually your own, or one run by someone you know). Nothing is sent to TaskHaven's maker.

Who is responsible

The TaskHaven app is made by [Your name]. Contact: [your contact email].

Your account and everything you put in the app are kept on the Vikunja server you connect it to, not by us. Whoever runs that server (you, a family member, your club or employer) is responsible for that data, and their own privacy terms apply. We can't see it, change it or delete it.

What the app sends to your server

  • Your account: your username, email address and password when you make an account or sign in, and the name you choose. People you share a project or team with see your name and username; the app doesn't show them your email.
  • What you put in the app: projects, tasks (with their dates, labels, priorities, descriptions and reminders), comments, files you attach, teams, labels, saved filters and your settings.

It goes straight from your phone to the server's address you typed: encrypted (https) for a server on the internet; for a server on your home network, as that server is set up (plain http is allowed there).

What stays on your phone

  • The server's address, and a session from the server that keeps you signed in (never your password).
  • A copy of what was last fetched, so the app opens without the server. Signing out, or removing the app, deletes them.
  • Reminders, set as notifications on the phone itself.

What we get

Nothing. TaskHaven has no servers of its own, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking, and doesn't use your contacts, location, camera or microphone. Files are only those you choose to attach. The demo keeps its made-up data on the phone, and only while it's open.

Who can see your data

  • You. Your projects are visible only to you, until you share one with someone on the server by their username, with a team, or by a link you make. Then they see its tasks, comments and files, and your name.
  • Whoever runs the server, as on any server.
  • Not us.

How long, and your rights

Your data stays on the server until you delete it, or your account. You can see and change it in the app, and delete your account in the app (Settings → Account → Delete account): the server emails you a link to confirm, then deletes it three days later. See Delete your account. For anything else about the data on a server (a copy of it, corrections, a server that can't send email), ask whoever runs it. Questions about the app itself: [your contact email].

Children

TaskHaven isn't meant for children under 16 on their own: an account for a child is a matter for the parent and whoever runs the server.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new date, and big changes are mentioned in the app.
