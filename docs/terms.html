These terms are for the TaskHaven app. They're short.

The app and your server

TaskHaven is an app for Vikunja servers: your account and your data are on the server you connect it to (your own, or one run by someone you know), and that server's own rules apply too. TaskHaven is an independent app: it isn't made by, or connected with, the Vikunja project. To use it you need to be 16 or older, or have a parent set it up with you. Keep your password to yourself; you're responsible for what's done with your account.

What you put in it

Your tasks, comments and files are yours. Don't put in anything that's illegal, or that harasses, threatens or exploits anyone, shares someone's private information without their consent, or infringes someone else's rights. Don't use TaskHaven to send spam, or try to get into projects or accounts that aren't yours.

Reporting someone

You decide who sees your projects: an admin can stop sharing a project with anyone (Share in a project's menu), and you can leave any team. If someone sent you something abusive or illegal, tell whoever runs your server (they can close the account), and keep a screenshot. You can also tell us on the app's GitHub page, github.com/bornaradusin/TaskHaven/issues (it's public: leave out names and anything private): we don't see what's on servers and can't remove it, but we'll help you with what to do next. If someone is in danger, contact the police.

The app

TaskHaven is free software (GPL-3.0-or-later), provided as it is. We do our best to make it work well and keep your data safe, but we can't promise it's free of mistakes, so keep backups of your server. As far as the law allows, we aren't liable for losses from using it. We may change these terms; big changes are mentioned in the app.
