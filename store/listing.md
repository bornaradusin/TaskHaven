**Short description** (80 at most)
To-do lists and projects for your family, on your own Vikunja server.

**Full description** (4,000 at most)

TaskHaven keeps your tasks and projects on your own server, and shares them only with the people you choose.

It works with Vikunja, the free, open-source task server you can run at home (on a NAS, a Raspberry Pi or a home server) or on the internet. Type its address, sign in or make an account, and that's it. Your projects are visible to you alone; share one with your partner by their username, and just the two of you see it. Nobody can browse the server's people, and nobody sees your email.

PROJECTS, YOUR WAY
• Four views of every project: a list, a table, a Kanban board and a Gantt chart
• Drag tasks to reorder them, move cards between columns, or stretch a task on the timeline
• Sub-projects, colours, favourites, archive and duplicate

TASKS WITH EVERYTHING
• Due, start and end dates, reminders, and repeating tasks (every week, every month, or "3 days from when it's done")
• Priorities, labels, progress, colours and checklists
• Assign people, comment, attach photos and files, and link related tasks (subtasks, blocking, duplicates)

QUICK ADD
Type "Take out the bins every tuesday at 7pm *chores @sam" and TaskHaven sets the date, the repeat, the label and the person for you.

TOGETHER, PRIVATELY
• Share a project by username: they can see, edit, or manage it
• Teams: share projects with a whole group at once
• Links that open a project in the server's web pages
• See when someone assigns you a task or comments on yours

YOUR DATA, YOUR SERVER
• Everything is on your server: nothing goes to TaskHaven's maker
• At home over your Wi-Fi, or anywhere through https (or a VPN such as Tailscale)
• Shows what it fetched last when the server can't be reached
• Two-step sign-in codes work too

ALSO
• Upcoming: what's due in the next days, overdue first
• Search everything, and save filters like "priority >= 3 && dueDate < now+7d"
• Reminders arrive even when the app is closed
• Light and dark theme
• A demo to look around without a server
• No ads, no tracking. Free and open source (GPL-3.0).

TaskHaven is an independent app that works with Vikunja servers. It isn't made by, or connected with, the Vikunja project.
